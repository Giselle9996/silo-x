Refunds and cancellations.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Custom Website Design

Cancel before we've started creative direction, and you get a full refund. Once that work has started, the $2,499 is non-refundable, no matter how far along the project is. We scope and price this as one project, not a pay-as-you-go arrangement, so refunds work the same way: whether work has actually started is what decides it, not how much of it is done.

Business Operations & Automation

The onboarding fee is non-refundable once your discovery/kickoff call happens, that's when the real work starts: mapping your processes and building your first workflows. Monthly subscriptions can be canceled anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, you keep access through the period you've already paid for, and we don't refund the unused part of a month.
