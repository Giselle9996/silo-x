Terms of Service

The basics of working with SiloX.

Custom Website Design

$2,499 gets you up to 5 pages, a responsive site, a contact form, advanced on-page SEO, basic analytics, 2 rounds of revisions, testing, and a proper handoff at launch. Anything outside that, more pages, ecommerce, custom integrations, a full brand identity, gets quoted separately before we start on it.

Business Operations & Automation

Every plan has a one-time onboarding fee and a monthly subscription, both laid out on the Business Operations page and confirmed again when you enroll. Your plan covers a set number of workflow executions and new workflows each month. Go past that, and we'll quote the extra separately.

What counts as "standard"

A standard workflow has a clear goal, a defined start and end, predictable inputs, and only a handful of connected systems. If it needs heavy custom logic, a pile of integrations, or someone reviewing it by hand constantly, that's not standard, we'll scope and price it on its own.

Payment

Everything runs through Stripe. The website package is paid in full up front. Operations plans start with the one-time onboarding fee, then roll into a monthly subscription that keeps going until you cancel it, see our billing terms for the details.

If these terms change

We'll update this page and tell active clients directly, not just bury it here and hope you check back.
