Subscription Billing Terms

How monthly billing works.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What you're agreeing to

Confirming your monthly plan means you're letting us charge the card on file once a month, at your plan's price, until you cancel. The onboarding fee is separate, a one-time charge you pay when you enroll, not part of the monthly subscription.

When you get charged

Your first charge happens the moment you confirm your subscription on the enrollment page, for the full monthly amount. There's no proration based on what day of the month you sign up, you're charged the same either way. Every charge after that lands on that same date each month.

Taxes

If sales tax applies, Stripe calculates it and shows it to you at checkout before you pay anything.

Cancellation

Email contact@silo-x.co to cancel. It takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, you keep access through the period you've already paid for, and we don't refund the unused part of a month. More on that in our refund policy.
