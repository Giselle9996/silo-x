Privacy Policy

How SiloX handles your information.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What we collect

Whatever you type into a form on this site, basically. When you send an inquiry, start a website project, or sign up for an operations plan, we get your name, your business name, your email, and whatever you tell us about the project. Stripe handles your payment info directly, we never see or store your full card number.

What we do with it

We use it to scope your project, run your payment through Stripe, and email you about your account. That's it. We don't sell it, and we're not building a mailing list to sell you other stuff later.

How long we keep it

If you're an active web support or maintenance client, we keep your information for as long as that relationship runs. For everyone else, we keep the basics, your name, email, what you paid, and what for, for as long as tax and financial recordkeeping requires, standard practice for any business accepting payment. We don't hold onto anything longer than that just to have it around.

If you're an operations client

We write down exactly which systems and permissions you've given us access to, and you can pull that access whenever you want. We'll never ask for your password through a form on this website.

Other companies involved

Stripe handles payments. If you're an operations client, we also touch whatever platforms you've given us permission to use while we're building and running your workflows. Beyond that, your information stays with us.

Questions

Email contact@silo-x.co and ask.
