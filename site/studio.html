Running a business should feel good, not like a second job on top of your actual job. One team handles both sides of that, how your business looks and how it runs.

The idea behind it

Freedom is the whole point of owning a business.

Your own schedule. Your own calls. Something that's actually yours instead of something you're renting out from a boss. That's usually the whole reason people start a business in the first place, right before admin and a bland website quietly eat it back up.

That's the freedom we're trying to hand back: design that actually looks like you, and operations that run without you standing over them.
